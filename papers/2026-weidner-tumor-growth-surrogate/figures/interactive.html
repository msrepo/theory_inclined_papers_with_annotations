Things to try

  1. Play with the defaults. The tumour stretches along white matter, slows at grey matter and stops at the fluid spaces.
  2. Compare the orange outline with the faint red. The outline is roughly what a scan shows; the faint red beyond it is the invisible tail.
  3. Make a diffuse tumour: raise D, lower ρ, Reset, Play. The tail √(D/ρ) grows and hidden spread reaches far beyond the visible edge.
  4. Make a compact tumour: lower D, raise ρ. The front is steep and the visible edge is close to where cells stop.
  5. Match visible sizes of a diffuse and a compact tumour and compare their "any cells" areas.
  6. Move the threshold. The tumour is unchanged; only what counts as visible moves.

A 2-D slice with made-up anatomy, in grid units, with explicit finite differences. Like the paper's model it leaves out mass effect, necrosis and treatment. The speed and tail readouts are exact for constant D in one dimension and approximate here.
